## Step-by-step configuration & verification checklist

### 1. Sender domain & SMTP credential

- [ ] Provision an transactional SMTP account (e.g. Postmark, SendGrid, Mailgun, SES, or H20's Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace relay) for an H20 sender address (e.g. `events@h20.gg` or `noreply@eventlocatie.h20.gg`).
- [ ] Publish and verify **SPF**, **DKIM**, and **DMARC** DNS records for the sender domain so messages pass alignment in Gmail and Outlook:

```bash
dig +short TXT <sender-domain>
dig +short TXT _dmarc.<sender-domain>
```

### 3. Manual delivery & duplicate-prevention test (before activating schedule)

- [ ] **Test 1 — Receipt email (`received`):**
  1. Submit a test booking using your own Gmail and Outlook test addresses (ensure no real customer rows without `notification:<id>:received` exist in the table first!).
  2. Click **Execute workflow** (`Manual` trigger) on Workflow D.
  3. Confirm **1** email (`H20 H20-XXXXXXXX — aanvraag ontvangen`) arrives in the **Inbox** (not Spam) with a working `${FRONTEND_ORIGIN}/#request=<id>&token=<token>` link.
  4. Confirm row `notification:<bookingId>:received` (`kind: "notification"`) is written in the Data Table.
- [ ] **Test 2 — Duplicate prevention on receipt:**
  1. Click **Execute workflow** a second time immediately without changing booking state.
  2. Confirm `Prepare mail` outputs **0 items** and **no duplicate email** is sent.
- [ ] **Test 3 — Approval email (`approved`):**
  1. Approve the test booking in `/admin/bookings` (`requestStatus` becomes `approved`).
  2. Click **Execute workflow** on Workflow D.
  3. Confirm **1** approval email (`H20 H20-XXXXXXXX — voorstel beoordeeld`) arrives, its personal link opens the approved explanation when signed in as that customer, and `notification:<bookingId>:approved` is recorded.
- [ ] **Test 4 — Duplicate prevention after approval:**
  1. Click **Execute workflow** once more.
  2. Confirm **0 emails** are sent (both `received` and `approved` receipts are already recorded).
