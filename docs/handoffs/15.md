# Handoff: #15 Descope online payments

## Decision summary

Online payments, deposits, and automated refunds are formally descoped for the initial release in [`docs/adr/0002-descope-online-payments.md`](../adr/0002-descope-online-payments.md).

- Live bookings in n8n (`B-public-api-and-proposals.json` and `C-employee-api.json`) represent non-binding concept proposals reviewed by H20 staff rather than paid reservations.
- Capacities, external H20 room calendars, custom catering, and `op aanvraag` items require staff confirmation and formal quotation/invoicing before payment is taken.
- Local demo payment/refund fields remain strictly demo-only (`h20-angular-demo-v1`).
- Future post-go-live integration guidance (e.g. Mollie iDEAL triggered only after staff approval and final price confirmation) is recorded in ADR 0002.

## What changed

- Added `docs/adr/0002-descope-online-payments.md`.
- Updated `scripts/check-requirements.cjs` to detect `docs/adr/0002-descope-online-payments.md` and report `BUITEN SCOPE (ADR): Online betalingen (docs/adr/0002-descope-online-payments.md)`.
- Added unit tests in `scripts/tests/check-requirements.test.cjs`.
