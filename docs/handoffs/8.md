## Deduplication & pricing rules

1. **`H20 Event Zaal` vs `Rabo Esports Stadium` deduplication rule (CRITICAL):**
   - Official H20 pages `https://eventlocatie.h20.gg/h20-event-zaal/` and `https://eventlocatie.h20.gg/rabo-esports-stadium-eventlocatie/` describe the same main stadium/event hall space at H20 Purmerend (`1.056 m²`, `44 × 24 m`, mainstage `22 × 7 × 6,5 m`, `50 m²` LED-panelen) under two marketing angles.
   - **Decision applied:** **`Rabo Esports Stadium`** is published as the single canonical active room (`isActive: true`, `50 – 1.200 personen`), while `h20-event-zaal` is stored with `isActive: false` so simultaneous bookings cannot double-book the same physical hall.
2. **Capacity verification (`capacityVerified`):**
   - Capacities and facilities are taken strictly from the official scraped `eventlocatie.h20.gg` pages (`Rabo Esports Stadium`: `50–1.200`, `H20 Lounge Café`: `tot 60 zitplekken`, `Aula`: `30–200 personen`).
3. **Unknown prices remain `op aanvraag` (`null`):**
   - All unconfirmed hourly room rates, per-person catering rates, and activity/upgrade tariffs are kept `null` (**op aanvraag**), which server validation and UI (`PR #55`) surface explicitly without inventing prices.
