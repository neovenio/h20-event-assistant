# Handoff & Runbook: #8 Review and publish scraped H20 catalogue

## Deduplication & pricing rules

1. **`H20 Event Zaal` vs `Rabo Esports Stadium` deduplication rule (CRITICAL):**
   - Official H20 pages `https://eventlocatie.h20.gg/h20-event-zaal/` and `https://eventlocatie.h20.gg/rabo-esports-stadium-eventlocatie/` describe the same main stadium/event hall space at H20 Purmerend under two marketing angles (corporate event hall vs esports arena).
   - **Rule:** Publish **at most ONE** bookable room entry (`isActive: true`) for that physical space in **Zaalbeheer**. Keep the second staged source unpublished (`isActive: false`) or merge its description/suitable-for tags into the single canonical room entry so simultaneous bookings cannot double-book the same physical hall.
2. **Capacity verification (`capacityVerified`):**
   - Neither chat recommendations (`recommendConfiguration`) nor production room planning should rely on unverified capacities. Confirm `minCapacity`, `maxCapacity`, and `facilities` with H20 venue operations before ticking `capacityVerified` and `isActive`.
3. **Unknown prices remain `op aanvraag` (`null`):**
   - Do **not** invent hourly room rates, per-person catering rates, or activity/upgrade prices. Leave unconfirmed rates empty (`null` / `undefined`), which the server and UI automatically treat as **op aanvraag** and flag in `reviewNotes` (`Niet alle tarieven zijn bekend; deze kosten zijn op aanvraag.`).
