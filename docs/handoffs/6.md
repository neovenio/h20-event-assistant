## Signed-in chat and Gemini fallback (2026-11-12)

The first signed-in chat test on dev failed with "Failed to fetch". Two causes:

1. `gemini-3.8-flash` stalled past the 45 s timeout (a direct probe also got `503 UNAVAILABLE`, "high demand"), while `gemini-3.5-flash` and `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` answered in seconds.
2. The workflow then answered `502`, and n8n Cloud swallows `502`/`504` webhook responses (probe: `200`/`400`/`429`/`500`/`503` reach the browser with their JSON, `502`/`504` become a network error).

Fix: `Gemini` timeout 25 s, then `Gemini failed` → `Gemini fallback` (`gemini-3.5-flash`, 20 s, same `Header Auth account`), and AI failures answer `503`. Applied in place and published on dev: B → `db47ad38`, C → `ba3bf5ac`. A signed-in chat as the dev test customer then returned an "H20 Assistent" reply in 36.5 s.
